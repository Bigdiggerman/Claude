# Marketing compliance check

Every piece of marketing collateral gets this check before it is signed off,
sent, printed, published or described as ready. It runs on collateral written
here, collateral handed over to proof, and collateral already in market.

If collateral is being checked for anything at all — wording, layout, numbers,
a typo — the licensing check runs too. It is never skipped because the ask was
narrow, and a piece is never called fine or finished without it.

## The required wording

```
Forestry Sales Limited, Licensed Agent REA Act 2008
```

- **Forestry Sales Limited** is the licensed agent. **Searle Forestry** is a
  trading style. Collateral may lead with Searle Forestry branding, but
  "Forestry Sales Limited" must still appear — s 121 requires the agent's name
  *and*, where the business is not carried on in that name, the style it is
  carried on under. Searle Forestry alone is a fail.
- REA accepts **"REA Act"** or **"REAA 2008"** as references to the Real Estate
  Agents Act 2008. "Licensed under the Real Estate Agents Act 2008" written out
  in full is also fine. Anything else is a fail — flag variants such as
  "REAA08", "Real Estate Act 2008", "Licensed REA", or the misspelling
  "licenced".
- It must be **legible at final size** — actual printed size, actual screen
  size, actual signboard viewing distance. Present but unreadable is a fail.
- It must be on **every piece and every page of a set**, not only the cover or
  the first page of a multi-page document.

## Where it has to appear

Section 121 of the Real Estate Agents Act 2008 requires the agent's name and
the fact that the agent is licensed to be displayed prominently on:

- every office or shop maintained for the business
- every website maintained for the business
- all notices, advertisements and other material published by or on behalf of
  the agent
- all letters, accounts, contractual documents, agreements and other documents
  sent, handed out, entered into or published in the course of the business

In practice that covers effectively everything that leaves the office: flyers,
marketing reports, IMs, tender and EOI packs and their covering letters, vendor
reports, listing copy on Trade Me and realestate.co.nz, signboards, email
signatures and campaigns, newsletters, social posts, print ads, business cards,
proposals and appraisals, agency agreements.

## The check

| # | Confirm | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Licence line is present | No mention of Forestry Sales Limited or of the Act |
| 2 | Wording is exact | Entity name wrong or missing; unapproved abbreviation; "licenced" |
| 3 | Legible at final size | Set in 4pt, reversed out of a photo, cropped at the trim, off the bottom of the artboard |
| 4 | On every page and every asset in the set | Cover carries it, inside pages don't; carousel slide 1 only |
| 5 | Salesperson is identifiable with the agency | Personal branding or a mobile number with no agency identification |
| 6 | No misleading or unsubstantiated claims (r 6.4) | "Guaranteed returns", yields or carbon revenue stated as fact without a source, superlatives that can't be backed |
| 7 | Photos and plans are current and of this property | Old imagery after harvest, windthrow, clearing or subdivision; stock or drone shots implying this site |
| 8 | Areas, stockings, ages, carbon and consent claims match the source | Net stocked area or NZU numbers that don't tie back to the report or registry they came from |
| 9 | Price and value representations are supported | An appraisal figure presented as a valuation, or a range with no basis |
| 10 | Agency agreement covers the marketing, and marketing stops when it ends (r 9.6) | Advertising still live after the agency period ended |
| 11 | Vendor has approved this version | Substantive change since the version the vendor saw |
| 12 | Contact details correct | Old phone, dead link, wrong salesperson |

## Reporting the result

Lead with a one-line verdict — **PASS** or **FIX BEFORE RELEASE** — then a
numbered list of fixes, each quoting the exact text and saying where it sits
(page, slide, field, footer). Give the corrected wording, don't just name the
problem.

State plainly what could not be checked: pages that wouldn't render, an image
with no readable text, a linked asset not supplied. Never let an unchecked page
sit inside a PASS.

## Collateral already in market

A missing or wrong licence line on something already published is a live
compliance exposure, not a note for next time. Say so, name every place it is
running, and give the correction for each.

## Limits

This is a working check against REA's published requirements, not legal advice.
Section 121 is the source of the display requirement; rule numbers cited are
from the Real Estate Agents Act (Professional Conduct and Client Care) Rules
2012. Where a piece raises something genuinely novel, say so and send it to the
office rather than signing it off.
